# Prompt examples

Replace bracketed text with your topic, constraints, and idea.

## Full landscape review

```text
Use $literature-landscape-review to build an auditable literature map of
[topic] through [date]. Cover canonical work, recent work, and adversarial
neighbors under different terminology. Report the query families, discovery
paths, saturation evidence, and remaining blind spots. End with unresolved
research gaps rather than merely summarizing papers.
```

## Novelty stress test

```text
Use $literature-landscape-review to stress-test this idea:
[idea]

Find the papers most likely to invalidate the novelty claim. Compare them by
problem, assumptions, mechanism, data or feedback, setting, evaluation, and
claimed outcome. State what I cannot claim as new, the narrowest contribution
that may remain defensible, the evidence required, and the main way the claim
could still fail.
```

## Research direction selection

```text
Use $literature-landscape-review to compare these candidate directions:
[direction A]
[direction B]
[direction C]

For each, assess literature maturity, closest prior work, unresolved bottleneck,
feasible contribution boundary, minimum falsification experiment, and likely
scientific or practical value. Do not equate an active literature with a dead
direction. Recommend a direction only after stating search uncertainty.
```

## Update an older review

```text
Use $literature-landscape-review to update the attached review from [old cutoff]
through today. Search with both its original vocabulary and terms introduced by
newer papers. Identify work that changes the taxonomy, invalidates an earlier
gap, or narrows the proposed contribution. Keep preprint and published versions
linked rather than double-counting them.
```

## Contribution and experiment design

```text
Use $literature-landscape-review to turn this broad idea into a defensible paper
claim. Trace each candidate contribution through:
difference -> mechanism -> expected consequence -> measurement -> value.

Produce a minimal evidence plan, a publication-grade plan, required baselines,
critical ablations, failure criteria, and claim wording calibrated to the
evidence.
```

## Short but rigorous answer

```text
Use $literature-landscape-review for a concise assessment of [idea]. Keep the
answer short, but do not omit the closest prior work, the defensible contribution
boundary, search cutoff, confidence, or remaining blind spots.
```
